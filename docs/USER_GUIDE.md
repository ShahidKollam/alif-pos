# Alif POS — User Guide (Phase 2)

## Billing (most important)

1. App opens on **Billing**
2. Press **W** for wholesale or **R** for retail
3. Type product name/code → **Enter** to add
4. Change quantities in the Qty column
5. Select customer (optional) — previous due appears automatically
6. Enter amount received
7. If paid more than the bill, tick **Keep as advance** only when the customer wants credit balance; otherwise treat as change
8. **Save Bill** — stock reduces and customer outstanding updates together

### Multiple bills at once

- **F8** or **Ctrl+T** or **+** on the tab bar opens another bill tab
- Each tab has its own mode, customer, items and totals
- Close a tab with the × on the tab (at least one tab always remains)

## Products

- **F2** or Products → Add Product
- Wholesale and retail prices are stored separately
- Deactivate instead of deleting products used on invoices

## Stock

- View current stock and low-stock items
- **Adjust** to increase or decrease with a note (purchase / damage / count)

## Customers & Ledger

- Register customers with optional opening outstanding
- **Ledger** shows sales, payments and running balance
- **Collect payment** records money against outstanding without a new sale

## Sales History

- Search by invoice number or customer
- Filter wholesale / retail
- Open any invoice to see line items

## Reports

- Snapshot: total sales, wholesale vs retail, outstanding, low stock

## Settings

- App version and paths
- Create / list / restore backups
- Load or reset **demo** sample data only
